# Matthias annex overrun findings

Source: /home/ubuntu/upload/Lifework-Matthias.pdf
Inspected pages: 20-24 via PDF view.

Findings:
- Page 20 (annex A / life history timeline) is visibly overrunning at the bottom. The next entry begins on the current page and spills into the footer area. Specifically, the next item after "Won a court case" starts with "wrote a law" and is cut off by the footer, indicating the life-history annex timeline page is not respecting page height.
- Page 21 (annex B / biographical data) is also overrunning. The educational-history list continues past the visible page boundary; the lower entries are cut off by the footer area.
- Page 22 (annex B / career history) appears very dense and may be close to capacity, but the visible issue is less severe than pages 20-21.
- Pages 23-24 (annex C / VIA pages) appear to fit within the page correctly.

Initial hypothesis:
- The page overruns are concentrated in annexes A and B, likely caused by uncapped item counts or uncapped text lengths in the annex data assembly rather than a global CSS issue.
- Annex A likely needs stricter pagination or per-page item caps for timeline events.
- Annex B likely needs stronger caps for education/career entry counts or text length trimming within education-history rows.
